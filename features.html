Exploration

Steal scooters and cars confirmed

Walk up to a passer-by on a scooter or in a car and press Triangle. The first press makes them get off, the second puts you at the wheel. The passer-by stays in town, and traffic no longer recycles the stolen vehicle, so you can leave it and find it where you parked it.

Drivable cars confirmed

In the original game you can only look at traffic cars. With the mod you can drive them, just like scooters.

Van and NOS confirmed

The vehicle menu recalls a drivable van, built from the van model already in town. Hold R1 while driving for NOS: double speed and a blue and orange flame from the exhaust.

Flying testing

Online, the host can enable unlimited flight and set its maximum height. Since 23 October single player uses the original game flight; this separation awaits confirmation after a fresh start.

Crocodiles: chase and bite confirmed

Playing as Aldo, R1 starts the chase and the bite. The prey stays in his mouth while the crocodile walks, even on a vehicle; press R1 again to let go.

Menu vehicles and Raoul confirmed

Skateboards and planes picked from the menu no longer disappear. Raoul the crow shows up in the vehicles menu.

Character switch testing

Hold L1 and press RIGHT to cycle through available characters while exploring (updated controls, testing).

Wardrobe testing

Press and release L1 alone to open or close the wardrobe (updated controls, testing). A button combination cancels the tap. Pick pieces (hats, masks, costumes) slot by slot with the arrows. The accessories come from the original game, where other characters wore them. Online, your choice is saved in your profile.

Day and night confirmed

A full cycle lasts 15 minutes. At night there are fewer people on the streets and the swamp music plays. The controller shortcut has been removed; day/night remains configurable by the host.

Split-screen co-op confirmed

Two players on the same console. With two players the horizon fog is removed. L3+R3 calls back a partner who is far away.

Quick boot confirmed

The three logo videos at startup are skipped and the game goes straight to the menu.

Online

How it works, rules and limits: see the Online page.

Launcher confirmed

An app for macOS and Windows sets up PCSX2 (memory, patches, the link to the game), starts the game and joins a server, or hosts one. You don't need to install Python.

In-game online menu testing

L1+UP opens the online menu without leaving the game: Host, Join, Disconnect. Leaving the game disconnects you from the server.

Persistent profiles and feathers confirmed

The server remembers each player's position, wardrobe, feathers and unlocked vehicles. Every player has their own feathers: dropped ones belong to whoever picks them up first.

Shared traffic confirmed

Everyone sees the same people and the same vehicles in the same places. Thefts show up in every client too.

Shared vehicles and passengers confirmed

A scooter used by one player is the same vehicle for everyone else. They see it start, lean into turns and stop, and they can hop on afterwards. You can also ride as a passenger.

Remote crocodile chase animation testing

The chase animation could stay frozen when seen by another player. A correction now preserves the remote movement data used to advance that animation. Targeted animation checks pass; visual confirmation with two players is pending. Testing requires a fresh game boot with the updated source launcher, without loading an older emulator state.

Rejoin from the game testing

After disconnecting and restoring the offline game, L1+UP → Entra reconnects to the last server with the launcher running in the background. The launcher recognises this connection and can disconnect it. Network or patch errors stay visible instead of asking you to load an already loaded game. Automated lifecycle checks pass; in-game confirmation is pending.

Campaign together testing

Story missions are played together. If one player wins, everyone wins; a player who strays too far or leaves makes everyone lose. Skipping a cutscene or confirming the end screen counts for everyone, and saving is skipped automatically. Missions 11 → 12 → 13 have been played online in a row.

Host settings testing

From the L1+UP menu the host can set starting feathers, unlocks (scooters, vehicles, items, maps, characters), the day and night cycle, passers-by on or off, and unlocked areas of town. The host can also reset a player's profile or shut the server down.

Modes, accessories, world items and pushing testing

Network schema 14, 22 October 2026. Campaign keeps the shared story and missions; Free Play disables mission starts, map markers and radar signs while preserving progress. The host can make every accessory available: turning the option off removes worn pieces the player does not own. The individual profile command grants or removes the entire accessory catalogue.

The host menu can place unlock/purchase points for scooters, vehicles, objects, maps, wearables and accessories, choosing a feather price, distance and height. Each player unlocks them separately; paid purchases require confirmation and repeated requests do not charge twice. Some abilities/maps use a feather placeholder. Display vehicles unlock their inventory recall.

On foot, L1 + Circle near a player/NPC in front of you requests a push, with a fall, an impulse in the kick's direction and dropped feathers. The skate animation is a candidate for reverse playback. Automated checks and a real network flow pass; visuals, feather amounts and collisions still need two-client in-game testing. Fully restart updated server and clients without older savestates.

Voice, walking speed and passenger controls testing

Press L1 + DOWN to toggle the microphone. It starts muted on every connection. The microphone icon at the bottom right is red when muted, green when enabled, brighter while speaking, and amber on an audio/permission error. Nearby players hear your voice with distance attenuation, up to 25 metres. Native Talk jaw tracks animate the local and visible remote character while leaving body movement free.

L1 + Triangle boards the rear seat of another player's or NPC's scooter/compatible vehicle; Circle still gets off. These combinations take priority over the wardrobe. Host walking-speed settings now multiply the actual native maximum speed, preserving the physics timestep and acceleration.

Audio uses the system's default input/output and requests microphone permission on activation. Up to four nearby speakers are audible together. Audio and jaw motion are implemented and automatically checked, but microphone hardware, two-computer latency, facial appearance and Windows still need testing.

Single player and server settings testing

Tests on 23 October found stationary offline NPCs and missing mission icons on foot. Two patch causes were corrected: stale NPC return branches and icon output data overwritten by the Free Play filter. The host’s unlimited-flight and height options now activate only online; single player uses the original flight. Server civilians, unlocks, accessories, speed, time and progress apply during the connection; the local story is restored on exit. Late packets cannot reapply the profile after restoration.

650 automated tests pass, one skipped. Check in game after a full restart: walking/driving NPCs, icons on foot/on vehicles and offline restoration after changing host options. Do not load older emulator states.
