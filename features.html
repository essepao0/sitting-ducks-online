Exploration

Steal scooters and cars confirmed

Walk up to a passer-by on a scooter or in a car and press Triangle. The first press makes them get off, the second puts you at the wheel. The passer-by stays in town, and traffic no longer recycles the stolen vehicle, so you can leave it and find it where you parked it.

Drivable cars confirmed

In the original game you can only look at traffic cars. With the mod you can drive them, just like scooters.

Van and NOS confirmed

L3+Select spawns a drivable van, built from the van model already in town. Hold R1 while driving for NOS: double speed and a blue and orange flame from the exhaust.

Flying confirmed

Bill can fly at low altitude (up to 9 m). Taking off from water uses the game's own flight.

Crocodiles: chase and bite confirmed

Playing as Aldo, R1 starts the chase and the bite. The prey stays in his mouth while the crocodile walks, even on a vehicle; press R1 again to let go.

Menu vehicles and Raoul confirmed

Skateboards and planes picked from the menu no longer disappear. Raoul the crow shows up in the vehicles menu.

Character switch confirmed

The left and right arrows switch between Bill and Aldo while exploring.

Wardrobe confirmed

L1 opens the wardrobe. Pick pieces (hats, masks, costumes) slot by slot with the arrows. The accessories come from the original game, where other characters wore them. Online, your choice is saved in your profile.

Day and night confirmed

A full cycle lasts 15 minutes. At night there are fewer people on the streets and the swamp music plays. R3+Select switches between day and night instantly.

Split-screen co-op confirmed

Two players on the same console. With two players the horizon fog is removed. L3+R3 calls back a partner who is far away.

Quick boot confirmed

The three logo videos at startup are skipped and the game goes straight to the menu.

Online

How it works, rules and limits: see the Online page.

Launcher confirmed

An app for macOS and Windows sets up PCSX2 (memory, patches, the link to the game), starts the game and joins a server, or hosts one. You don't need to install Python.

In-game online menu confirmed

R3+UP opens the online menu without leaving the game: Host, Join, Disconnect. Leaving the game disconnects you from the server.

Persistent profiles and feathers confirmed

The server remembers each player's position, wardrobe, feathers and unlocked vehicles. Every player has their own feathers: dropped ones belong to whoever picks them up first.

Shared traffic confirmed

Everyone sees the same people and the same vehicles in the same places. Thefts show up in every client too.

Shared vehicles and passengers confirmed

A scooter used by one player is the same vehicle for everyone else. They see it start, lean into turns and stop, and they can hop on afterwards. You can also ride as a passenger.

Campaign together testing

Story missions are played together. If one player wins, everyone wins; a player who strays too far or leaves makes everyone lose. Skipping a cutscene or confirming the end screen counts for everyone, and saving is skipped automatically. Missions 11 → 12 → 13 have been played online in a row.

Host settings testing

From the R3+UP menu the host can set starting feathers, unlocks (scooters, vehicles, items, maps, characters), the day and night cycle, passers-by on or off, and unlocked areas of town. The host can also reset a player's profile or shut the server down.
