Mission

Why I'm doing this. Sitting Ducks is a small, forgotten game from 2003, built on Asobo's Zouna engine. Ducktown is a living town full of ducks, crocodiles, scooters and shops, but you explore it alone. I wanted to explore it with friends.

So we started taking it apart. First came split-screen co-op, then stealing a scooter from a passer-by, then cars, the van and flying. Finally came online play, with the story campaign played together.

The project is also a journal. Every discovery about the engine (memory addresses, structures, functions) is documented on the Game engine page, so anyone can understand how it works and carry the work on.

What it is not: the mod does not include the game. You need your own copy (PS2, European release, SLES-52116) and your console's BIOS. We do not distribute ISOs, BIOS files or ELFs.
